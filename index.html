Concept 2
Digital Nostalgia
ASCII art portraits with CRT scan lines, blue phosphor glow, and VHS tracking artifacts. Behind the digital noise, a human emerges.
Concept 3A
Halftone Blue
Blue monochrome halftone dots on cream with a blueprint grid. Dots scatter into diagonal hatching patterns between portraits. Editorial and upmarket.
Concept 3B
CMYK Halftone
Four-color separation halftone with Maven blue-dominant Cyan channel. Channels misregister during transitions like a printing press aligning. Newspaper meets digital.
